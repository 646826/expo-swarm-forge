Field guide

How to play

  1. Swap neighbors. Match three or more identical charms in a row.
  2. Forge power. Four creates a row or column firecracker. Five, T or L creates dynamite.
  3. Chain cascades. Falling charms can match again and multiply your reward.
  4. Beat sunset. Reach 5,000 points before all 20 moves are spent.
